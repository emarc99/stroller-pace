⚠️ The "Pocket-Cadence vs Wheel-Rumble" Failure Engineered Out:

In loose running pockets, natural leg swing at 2.6–3.0 Hz generated large vertical amplitudes (>1.5g). Naive peak detectors triggered a 38.4% false alarm rate. With our 4th-order Butterworth high-pass filter ($f_c = 6.0\text{ Hz}$), false alarms dropped to 2.1% (an 18.3x improvement).
